# Harvest Privacy Policy — DRAFT (have a lawyer review before publishing)

Last updated: <date>. Operated by: <legal owner name, address, contact email>.

**The short version.** Harvest keeps your cards and your price-watch list on your phone. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, card numbers or which cards you have.

**What stays on your phone.** The bank, card network and type of cards you add (never the card number), the UPI/wallet apps you tick, and your price-watch list. Cards are unlocked with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode.

**What we receive.** When you search, we receive the words you searched for. We do not receive which cards or apps you have; matching happens on your phone. We keep anonymous counts of searches and which stores people open. These are not linked to you.

**Price watch.** Checks run from your phone as ordinary searches. We do not know what you are watching.

**Affiliate links.** When you tap "Buy", you go to the store through a link that may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. The store may use its own cookies; see its policy.

**Your choices.** "Wipe my data" in the app removes everything stored on your phone. Uninstalling does the same.

**Children.** Harvest is not for people under 18.
