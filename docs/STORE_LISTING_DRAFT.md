# Store listing draft

**Name:** Harvest: Card Offer Finder
**Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Best price for your cards
**Short description (Play, 80):** Find the lowest price for what you buy, using the cards you already own.

**Long description**
Search a product. Harvest shows each store's price, then works out what you'd really pay with your own cards and UPI apps. Your cards never leave your phone: we only ever save the bank, network and type, never the number. Watch a price and get a nudge when it drops.

- Real price after bank, card, UPI and coupon offers
- Your cards stay on this phone, unlocked with Face ID / fingerprint
- Price history, so you can spot inflated "sale" prices
- Price watch with alerts

**App Review notes (Apple):** The app compares prices and offers across stores. No account is needed. Cards are not payment instruments in the app; no card numbers are collected. Purchases happen on the store's own site.
**Data safety / privacy labels:** Search terms (anonymous, not linked to identity); no contact info, no financial info collected.
**Category:** Shopping. **Age rating:** 4+ / Everyone.
